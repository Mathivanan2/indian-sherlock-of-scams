THE DETECTIVE BEHIND THE DESK

The case file on me

Every good detective story needs a narrator, and this one's mine. By day, I'm buried in the kind of numbers that most people scroll past — ledgers, transaction trails, the fine print nobody reads until something goes wrong. By evening, I turn those same instincts toward something more fun: writing about the scams, frauds, and AI-shaped tricks reshaping how people lose money without ever noticing the door was open.

This blog started as a hobby — a place to think out loud about the patterns I kept noticing, the questions I couldn't stop asking, and the strange comfort in laying out a mystery clearly enough that anyone could follow the trail. No badge, no official case number. Just curiosity, a magnifying glass, and a soft spot for a good plot twist.

I don't chase criminals. I chase the moment right before someone almost falls for it — because that's the moment worth writing about.

When I'm not untangling a case file, you'll probably find me reading about the next big AI headline, arguing with myself over whether a scam is clever or just cruel, or hunting for the next story worth telling here.
